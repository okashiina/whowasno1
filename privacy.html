Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 (scheduling added)

What this covers

"Who Was No.1 Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Who Was No.1 YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else. This policy explains what the tool does with Google account data.

Data the tool accesses

  • Upload permission (YouTube scope youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos as private, with a scheduled publish time the owner can cancel.
  • Video settings (scope youtube): only to set or cancel the scheduled publish time of videos the tool uploaded. It never edits, deletes or comments on anything else.
  • Read-only channel information (scope youtube.readonly): the channel name and ID, to confirm the right channel is signed in, and the privacy status of videos the tool uploaded.

The tool does not read email, contacts, comments, subscribers, viewing history or any other account data.

Storage and sharing

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Viewers of the channel

The tool collects no data about people who watch Who Was No.1 videos. YouTube's own handling of viewer data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also ends the tool's access.
